Some of the work I care most about was never a product. It was people building something together.

## Open Software Network (AYA)

In February 2023, two earthquakes hit southern Türkiye. Within hours, developers started volunteering to help, and I co-founded the **Open Software Network (AYA)** to organise them. It grew to **25,000+ volunteer developers**.

- **[AfetHarita](https://afetharita.com)**: an emergency aid map that turned calls for help into locations rescue teams could act on. **AFAD**, Türkiye's disaster and emergency agency, used it.
- **[deprem.io](https://deprem.io)**: an earthquake relief platform built by the same network.
- The work was covered by [TIME](https://time.com/6254500/turkey-earthquake-twitter-musk-rescue/) and [NBC San Diego](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/video/news/local/rancho-penasquitos-man-working-with-thousands-online-to-help-turkey-earthquake-victims/3170373/).
